Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

Buffaloes News Radar rclone is a personal application used only by its owner. It transfers the owner's archived news data to the owner's Google Drive through rclone and GitHub Actions.

Google user data

The application accesses Google Drive only after the owner grants permission. Access is used solely to create, read, verify, and maintain the owner's archive files.

Data sharing

Google user data is not sold, shared with unrelated third parties, or used for advertising.

Credentials

OAuth tokens and configuration credentials are stored as encrypted secrets and are not published in this website or its source repository.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the owner's Google Account security settings.
